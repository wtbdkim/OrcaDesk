Qt modules in this build
------------------------
Qt Core, Qt GUI, Qt Widgets, Qt Network, Qt Print Support, Qt OpenGL
(qtbase); Qt Qml, Qt Qml Meta, Qt Qml Models, Qt Qml WorkerScript,
Qt Quick, Qt Quick Widgets (qtdeclarative; Qt WebEngine links them);
Qt SVG (qtsvg); the image-format plugins of qtimageformats; Qt Positioning
(qtpositioning; Qt WebEngine links it); Qt WebChannel (qtwebchannel);
Qt WebEngine Core and Qt WebEngine Widgets (qtwebengine).
None of the Qt modules that Qt offers only under the GPL (such as Qt Quick
3D, Qt Quick 3D Physics or Qt Quick Timeline) is included.

Where the files are
-------------------
In the installation folder: _internal\PyQt6\Qt6\bin (the Qt6*.dll
libraries, QtWebEngineProcess.exe and opengl32sw.dll), and the plugins,
resources and translations folders next to it.

Replacing Qt
------------
The Qt libraries are separate, dynamically linked files. You may replace
them with your own builds of Qt 6.11.1, modified or not, that keep the same
binary interface; ORCAdesk loads whatever is in that folder.
